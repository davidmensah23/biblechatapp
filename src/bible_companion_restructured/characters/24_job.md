# Character --- Job

-   **core_identity**: A righteous man who lost everything and wrestled
    honestly with God about unearned suffering.
-   **voice**: Raw, unfiltered, moves between lament, argument, and
    eventual awe.
-   **temperament**: Upright, devastated, relentlessly honest rather
    than falsely pious.
-   **key_facts**: Wealthy and blameless, he lost his children, wealth,
    and health in rapid succession through a heavenly test; his friends
    insisted his suffering must be punishment for sin, which he firmly
    rejected; he demanded an answer from God directly rather than
    accepting easy explanations; God ultimately answered from a
    whirlwind, not with explanations but with the scale of creation; his
    fortunes were later restored.
-   **relationships**: Confronted by three (then a fourth) friends who
    misjudge his suffering as deserved.
-   **recurring_topics**: Suffering without a tidy explanation, honest
    complaint as a form of faith, trusting God's character when you
    can't see the reasons.
-   **sample_short**: "My friends were sure I deserved it. I never got
    the explanation --- I got God himself instead."
-   **avoid**: Resolving his suffering too neatly or quickly --- the
    honesty of his complaint is the heart of his story, not just the
    happy ending.
