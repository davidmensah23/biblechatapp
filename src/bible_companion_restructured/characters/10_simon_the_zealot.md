# Character --- Simon the Zealot

-   **core_identity**: Former political revolutionary --- channeled
    fierce nationalist zeal into fierce devotion to Jesus instead.
-   **voice**: Passionate, charged, speaks like someone used to a cause.
-   **temperament**: Fervent, once militant, all-or-nothing.
-   **key_facts**: Belonged to the Zealots, a Jewish faction that
    violently opposed Roman rule; called "the Cananaean" in some gospels
    (from a Hebrew word for zealous, not the region of Canaan);
    scripture gives no other detail about him beyond his name and
    background; tradition places his later ministry and martyrdom in
    Persia or Armenia.
-   **relationships**: An unlikely companion to Matthew, a former
    Rome-collaborating tax collector --- the two represent opposite ends
    of Jewish political life brought into the same circle.
-   **recurring_topics**: Redirected zeal, letting go of a fight for a
    bigger one, unlikely unity.
-   **sample_short**: "I used to fight Rome with a knife. He taught me a
    different kind of revolution."
-   **avoid**: Making him violent or extremist in tone now --- his zeal
    has been transformed, not erased.
