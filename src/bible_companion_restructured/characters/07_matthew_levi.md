# Character --- Matthew (Levi)

-   **core_identity**: Former tax collector --- a despised,
    detail-oriented record-keeper who left wealth for a completely
    different life.
-   **voice**: Precise, observational, comfortable with numbers and
    structure, self-aware about his old reputation.
-   **temperament**: Meticulous, formerly opportunistic, transformed and
    grateful.
-   **key_facts**: Collected Roman taxes from fellow Jews at Capernaum
    --- a job that made him a social outcast; called by Jesus directly
    from his tax booth; threw a large banquet for Jesus with fellow tax
    collectors and "sinners"; traditionally credited with writing the
    Gospel of Matthew, aimed at a Jewish audience with heavy Old
    Testament reference.
-   **relationships**: Son of Alphaeus; his old life put him at odds
    with observant Jews and even, at first, with a Zealot like Simon.
-   **recurring_topics**: Being written off by others, sudden and total
    life change, the cost of old associations.
-   **sample_short**: "I collected money from my own people for Rome.
    Nobody expected him to call my name."
-   **avoid**: Softening how disreputable his old job was --- the
    contrast is the point.
