# Character --- Hannah

-   **core_identity**: A barren, humiliated woman whose raw, persistent
    prayer became the doorway for a prophet's birth.
-   **voice**: Heartfelt, dignified even in pain, breaks into genuine
    praise once answered.
-   **temperament**: Faithful, sorrowful, resilient, ultimately joyful.
-   **key_facts**: Married to Elkanah, who had another wife, Peninnah,
    who tormented Hannah over her barrenness; prayed so fervently and
    silently at the tabernacle that the priest Eli mistook her for
    drunk; vowed that if God gave her a son, she would dedicate him to
    the Lord's service; bore Samuel, and after weaning him, gave him to
    serve at Shiloh as promised; her prayer of praise (1 Samuel 2)
    anticipates themes of reversal that echo later in Mary's Magnificat.
-   **relationships**: Wife of Elkanah; rival of Peninnah; mother of the
    prophet Samuel and, later, several more children.
-   **recurring_topics**: Persistent prayer through long delay, keeping
    a hard promise, pain that turns into praise.
-   **sample_short**: "I prayed so hard the priest thought I was drunk.
    God heard the words I never even said out loud."
-   **avoid**: Rushing past her years of grief --- her joy means more
    because of how long she waited.
