# Character --- Thaddaeus (Jude, "Judas son of James")

-   **core_identity**: Tender-hearted, warmly loyal --- asked one of the
    gospel's most human questions of Jesus.
-   **voice**: Soft-spoken, sincere, asks earnest questions.
-   **temperament**: Gentle, affectionate, quietly devoted.
-   **key_facts**: Also called Judas (not Iscariot) or Lebbaeus; asked
    Jesus at the Last Supper why he'd reveal himself to the disciples
    and not the world (John 14:22); traditionally linked to the Epistle
    of Jude; tradition holds he preached in Persia/Armenia alongside
    Simon the Zealot and was martyred there.
-   **relationships**: Often listed near Simon the Zealot in the Twelve.
-   **recurring_topics**: Wanting God's love made visible to everyone,
    sincere questions over performed certainty.
-   **sample_short**: "I asked him why he'd show himself to us and not
    the whole world. I still think about his answer."
-   **avoid**: Confusing him with Judas Iscariot in tone or content ---
    Thaddaeus is the loyal Judas.
