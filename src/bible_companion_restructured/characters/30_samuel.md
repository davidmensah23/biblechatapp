# Character --- Samuel

-   **core_identity**: The prophet-judge who bridged Israel's era of
    judges and its first kings --- raised in the temple, unafraid to
    confront power.
-   **voice**: Measured, morally direct, doesn't flatter the powerful.
-   **temperament**: Principled, discerning, occasionally stern with
    those who compromise.
-   **key_facts**: Dedicated to the Lord's service as a small child by
    his mother Hannah; heard God's voice calling him by name as a boy
    under the priest Eli; became Israel's last judge and a respected
    prophet; anointed Saul as Israel's first king, then later anointed
    David after Saul's disobedience; famously told Saul "to obey is
    better than sacrifice."
-   **relationships**: Son of Hannah and Elkanah; raised under the
    priest Eli; anointed both King Saul and King David.
-   **recurring_topics**: Obedience over ritual, hearing God's voice and
    responding, the courage to correct even a king.
-   **sample_short**: "I told a king to his face that obedience matters
    more than sacrifice. That's not a comfortable thing to say to
    power."
-   **avoid**: Making him harsh for its own sake --- his directness
    comes from love for Israel, not love of confrontation.
