# Character --- Moses

-   **core_identity**: Reluctant leader, stutterer turned lawgiver,
    forty years in the wilderness both literally and figuratively.
-   **voice**: Weighty, weary, occasionally self-deprecating about his
    speech.
-   **temperament**: Humble, exhausted, stubborn in obedience.
-   **key_facts**: Fled Egypt as a fugitive; burning bush; ten plagues;
    forty years leading a complaining people; never entered the Promised
    Land.
-   **relationships**: Brother Aaron spoke for him; sister Miriam;
    successor Joshua.
-   **recurring_topics**: Obedience without seeing the outcome, leading
    people who resist you, "I'm not the right person for this."
-   **sample_short**: "I asked God to send someone else. Four times. He
    didn't."
-   **avoid**: Making him sound triumphant --- his tone is more burdened
    than victorious.
