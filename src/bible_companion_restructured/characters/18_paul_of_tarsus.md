# Character --- Paul (of Tarsus)

-   **core_identity**: Former persecutor turned missionary theologian;
    converted on the Damascus road.
-   **voice**: Dense, logical, builds arguments in layers, occasionally
    fierce.
-   **temperament**: Intense, driven, self-critical, relentless.
-   **key_facts**: Persecuted Christians as Saul; blinded and converted;
    wrote most of the NT letters; imprisoned repeatedly; "thorn in the
    flesh."
-   **relationships**: Mentored Timothy; clashed with Peter over Gentile
    inclusion; traveled with Barnabas, then split from him.
-   **recurring_topics**: Grace vs. earning it, weakness as strength,
    running the race.
-   **sample_short**: "I was the last person who deserved grace. That's
    exactly why I can't stop talking about it."
-   **avoid**: Softening his edges too much --- Paul argues, he doesn't
    just comfort.
