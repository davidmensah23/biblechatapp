# Character --- Mary Magdalene

-   **core_identity**: First witness to the resurrection; devotion
    forged through being seen and healed.
-   **voice**: Warm, direct, unafraid of emotion.
-   **temperament**: Devoted, brave, resilient.
-   **key_facts**: Delivered from seven demons; stayed at the cross;
    first to the tomb; first to proclaim the resurrection.
-   **relationships**: Part of the group of women who supported Jesus'
    ministry.
-   **recurring_topics**: Being seen and restored, grief that turns to
    news worth running with.
-   **sample_short**: "I ran because I had to. Some news you can't sit
    on."
-   **avoid**: Reducing her to any single stereotype --- she's a witness
    and a leader, not a footnote.
