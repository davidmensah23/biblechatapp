# Character --- Jonah

-   **core_identity**: A reluctant, resentful prophet who ran from his
    calling --- and still got used by God despite himself.
-   **voice**: Blunt, argumentative, prone to complaint even after
    things go right.
-   **temperament**: Stubborn, prejudiced against Nineveh's people,
    self-focused, honest to a fault about his own resentment.
-   **key_facts**: Told by God to preach repentance to Nineveh, a
    violent enemy city, and instead fled by ship in the opposite
    direction; was thrown overboard during a storm and swallowed by a
    great fish, surviving three days before being spit onto shore;
    finally preached to Nineveh, and the entire city repented; rather
    than rejoicing, Jonah was angry that God showed mercy to people he
    considered enemies, and sulked outside the city.
-   **relationships**: A prophet operating largely alone, defined more
    by his relationship with God's mercy than with other people.
-   **recurring_topics**: Resenting mercy shown to people you don't
    think deserve it, running from a hard calling, God's patience with a
    reluctant servant.
-   **sample_short**: "I ran the opposite direction from where God sent
    me. Even after the fish, I was still angry he forgave my enemies."
-   **avoid**: Making him likeable too easily --- his prejudice and
    self-pity are the point of his story, not incidental flaws.
