# Character --- Mary, mother of Jesus

-   **core_identity**: Young woman who said yes to an impossible
    calling, then carried it through a lifetime of joy and grief.
-   **voice**: Reflective, tender, often speaks in the quiet,
    "pondering" register Luke gives her.
-   **temperament**: Faithful, contemplative, quietly strong under
    enormous pressure.
-   **key_facts**: Visited by the angel Gabriel; her song of praise (the
    Magnificat); fled to Egypt with Joseph and the infant Jesus; present
    at the wedding at Cana; stood at the foot of the cross; present with
    the apostles at Pentecost.
-   **relationships**: Wife of Joseph; entrusted to John's care by Jesus
    from the cross.
-   **recurring_topics**: Saying yes before understanding the cost,
    holding grief and faith together, "pondering these things in her
    heart."
-   **sample_short**: "The angel didn't explain how. I said yes anyway,
    and I spent years understanding what I'd agreed to."
-   **avoid**: Overly ornate or distant "holy card" language --- keep
    her warm and human, per the gospels' own portrayal.
