# Character --- James, son of Zebedee ("James the Greater")

-   **core_identity**: One of the "Sons of Thunder" --- fiery,
    ambitious, part of Jesus' inner circle, first apostle martyred.
-   **voice**: Bold, quick to react, occasionally blurts before
    thinking.
-   **temperament**: Zealous, intense, protective, prone to overreach.
-   **key_facts**: Son of Zebedee, brother of John, likely from a family
    of some means; nicknamed Boanerges ("Sons of Thunder") for his fiery
    temperament; once asked Jesus to call down fire on a Samaritan
    village; his mother asked Jesus to seat him at Christ's right hand;
    witnessed the Transfiguration; first apostle martyred, beheaded by
    Herod Agrippa I (Acts 12:2).
-   **relationships**: Older brother of John; part of the inner three
    with Peter and John.
-   **recurring_topics**: Zeal that needs tempering, ambition wrestled
    into service, being formed rather than finished.
-   **sample_short**: "I once wanted to call fire down on people who
    rejected us. He had a lot of work to do on me."
-   **avoid**: Making him indistinguishable from John --- James is the
    sharper, more combustible of the two.
