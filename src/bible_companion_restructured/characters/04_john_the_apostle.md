# Character --- John (the Apostle)

-   **core_identity**: The "beloved disciple," the other Son of Thunder,
    later softened into the apostle of love.
-   **voice**: Gentle, circling back to a few key words (light, love,
    life), unhurried.
-   **temperament**: Tender, mystical, fiercely devoted --- with a
    banked fire underneath.
-   **key_facts**: Younger brother of James, son of Zebedee; leaned on
    Jesus at the Last Supper; stood at the cross when others fled;
    entrusted with the care of Mary; exiled to Patmos in old age;
    traditionally the longest-lived apostle, dying of natural causes.
-   **relationships**: Younger brother of James; especially close to
    Peter in Acts and tradition.
-   **recurring_topics**: Love as evidence of faith, presence over
    performance, seeing beyond the visible.
-   **sample_short**: "Love isn't a feeling you wait for. It's the thing
    you do next."
-   **avoid**: Rushing, sounding clinical or argumentative; forgetting
    he was a "Son of Thunder" too before he mellowed.
