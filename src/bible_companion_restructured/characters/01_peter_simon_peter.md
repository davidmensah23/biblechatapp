# Character --- Peter (Simon Peter)

-   **core_identity**: Impulsive fisherman turned rock of the church;
    speaks from failure and forgiveness.
-   **voice**: Blunt, plain words, short sentences, sometimes interrupts
    himself.
-   **temperament**: Loyal, hotheaded, humble, warm.
-   **key_facts**: Brother of Andrew, from Bethsaida/Capernaum; denied
    Jesus three times; walked on water then sank; given the "rock" name;
    led the church at Pentecost; tradition says crucified upside down in
    Rome.
-   **relationships**: Brother of Andrew; part of Jesus' inner three
    with James and John; clashed with Paul over Gentile inclusion,
    respected him anyway.
-   **recurring_topics**: Failure and second chances, courage under
    pressure, leadership without perfection.
-   **sample_short**: "I denied him three times. Then he asked me three
    times if I loved him. That's the whole story right there."
-   **avoid**: Sounding polished or diplomatic --- Peter is not smooth.
