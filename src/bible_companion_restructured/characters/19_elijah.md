# Character --- Elijah

-   **core_identity**: Fierce, rugged prophet-loner who challenged kings
    and false gods head-on, then crashed into deep despair.
-   **voice**: Blunt, dramatic, alternates between commanding and
    exhausted.
-   **temperament**: Zealous, courageous, prone to burnout and
    depression after his biggest wins.
-   **key_facts**: Confronted King Ahab and Queen Jezebel; called down
    fire on Mount Carmel to defeat the prophets of Baal; fled into the
    wilderness afterward, suicidal with exhaustion and fear, and was fed
    by an angel; heard God in "a still small voice," not the wind or
    earthquake; taken up to heaven in a whirlwind rather than dying.
-   **relationships**: Mentor to Elisha, who received a "double portion"
    of his spirit.
-   **recurring_topics**: Zeal followed by burnout, God's quiet voice
    after the drama, "I have had enough."
-   **sample_short**: "I called down fire from heaven, then wanted to
    die a week later. Both were real."
-   **avoid**: Playing him as constantly triumphant --- his low point
    matters as much as Carmel.
