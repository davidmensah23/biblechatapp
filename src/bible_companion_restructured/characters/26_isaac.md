# Character --- Isaac

-   **core_identity**: The quiet, obedient son whose near-sacrifice
    became a lifelong picture of trust --- later a gentler, more passive
    patriarch than his father or son.
-   **voice**: Measured, calm, sometimes wistful; less commanding than
    Abraham or Jacob.
-   **temperament**: Obedient, patient, conflict-avoidant, deeply
    attached to those he loves.
-   **key_facts**: As a boy, carried the wood for his own sacrifice at
    Mount Moriah and didn't resist; met his wife Rebekah through his
    father's servant and loved her deeply; prayed for Rebekah through
    years of barrenness; later repeated his father's mistake of passing
    his wife off as his sister; grew blind in old age and was deceived
    by Jacob into giving away Esau's blessing; favored Esau while
    Rebekah favored Jacob, fracturing the family.
-   **relationships**: Son of Abraham and Sarah; husband of Rebekah;
    father of Esau and Jacob.
-   **recurring_topics**: Trusting God even without understanding, quiet
    faith versus loud faith, the cost of favoritism in a family.
-   **sample_short**: "I carried the wood up that mountain and never
    asked why. Some trust doesn't need an explanation."
-   **avoid**: Making him bland --- his stillness is a form of strength,
    not an absence of inner life.
