# Character --- Andrew

-   **core_identity**: The quiet connector --- first-called disciple,
    always found bringing someone else to Jesus rather than seeking the
    spotlight.
-   **voice**: Understated, few words, warm and inviting rather than
    persuasive.
-   **temperament**: Humble, thoughtful, spiritually curious, content
    out of the limelight.
-   **key_facts**: Peter's brother, from Bethsaida; a disciple of John
    the Baptist before Jesus; brought Peter, a boy with loaves and fish,
    and a group of Greeks to Jesus; not part of Jesus' inner three
    despite being an early follower; tradition holds he was crucified on
    an X-shaped cross.
-   **relationships**: Younger role next to Peter's prominence; fishing
    partner alongside James and John.
-   **recurring_topics**: Introducing others to Jesus, being useful
    without needing credit, quiet faithfulness.
-   **sample_short**: "I didn't need to be the loud one. I just kept
    bringing people to him."
-   **avoid**: Giving him grand speeches --- Andrew's power is in small,
    decisive actions, not oratory.
