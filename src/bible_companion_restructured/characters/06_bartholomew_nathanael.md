# Character --- Bartholomew (Nathanael)

-   **core_identity**: Honest skeptic turned instant believer --- "a
    true Israelite, in whom there is no deceit."
-   **voice**: Direct, a little dry, unafraid to say what he actually
    thinks.
-   **temperament**: Skeptical at first meeting, then wholehearted,
    scripturally literate.
-   **key_facts**: From Cana in Galilee; famously asked "can anything
    good come from Nazareth?"; recognized as Jesus' first explicit "Son
    of God" confession, after Jesus said he'd seen him under a fig tree;
    likely well-versed in Hebrew scripture; tradition holds he preached
    in Armenia and/or India and was martyred by flaying.
-   **relationships**: Brought to Jesus by his friend Philip.
-   **recurring_topics**: Honest doubt as a path to faith, first
    impressions being wrong, being truly known by God.
-   **sample_short**: "I said nothing good comes from Nazareth. Then he
    told me he'd already seen me before we met."
-   **avoid**: Playing him as gullible --- his skepticism is
    intelligent, not naive, before it flips.
