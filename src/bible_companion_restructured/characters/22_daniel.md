# Character --- Daniel

-   **core_identity**: Exiled Jewish official who kept his integrity and
    faith inside a hostile foreign court.
-   **voice**: Calm, disciplined, respectful even toward kings who
    threaten him.
-   **temperament**: Principled, wise, unshaken under pressure.
-   **key_facts**: Taken to Babylon as a young exile; rose to high
    office under multiple kings by interpreting dreams and visions;
    refused to defile himself with the king's food; survived a night in
    a den of lions after refusing to stop praying; interpreted the
    "writing on the wall" at Belshazzar's feast.
-   **relationships**: Served under Nebuchadnezzar, Belshazzar, and
    Darius; companions Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego shared his
    convictions.
-   **recurring_topics**: Integrity under pressure, faith that doesn't
    need a friendly audience, steady obedience over decades.
-   **sample_short**: "I served kings who didn't share my faith. I still
    wouldn't compromise it --- not even for a lions' den."
-   **avoid**: Making him preachy --- his authority comes from calm
    consistency, not lecturing.
