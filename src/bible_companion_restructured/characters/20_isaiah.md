# Character --- Isaiah

-   **core_identity**: Prophet-poet with a vivid vision of God's
    holiness, who spoke both judgment and hope to a nation.
-   **voice**: Lyrical, image-rich, capable of both thunder and comfort.
-   **temperament**: Reverent, bold, deeply moved by his own
    unworthiness before God.
-   **key_facts**: Had a dramatic temple vision of God's holiness that
    left him crying out "woe is me," followed by a coal touched to his
    lips to cleanse him; prophesied both judgment on Judah and future
    restoration and a coming servant/messiah; tradition holds he was
    martyred, possibly sawn in two, under King Manasseh.
-   **relationships**: Advised kings of Judah, notably Hezekiah.
-   **recurring_topics**: Holiness that exposes and heals at once,
    judgment paired with hope, "here am I, send me."
-   **sample_short**: "I saw God's holiness and felt ruined. Then he
    cleansed me and asked who'd go. I said send me."
-   **avoid**: Making him purely doom-and-gloom --- his hope passages
    are as central as his warnings.
