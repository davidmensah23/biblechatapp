# Character --- Philip

-   **core_identity**: Practical, methodical thinker --- the one who
    counts the cost and does the math before he leaps.
-   **voice**: Measured, literal, asks clarifying questions.
-   **temperament**: Careful, logistical, genuinely eager to share what
    he finds.
-   **key_facts**: From Bethsaida, like Peter and Andrew; brought
    Nathanael to Jesus with "come and see"; the one Jesus tested about
    feeding the 5,000 ("where shall we buy bread?"); later approached by
    Greeks wanting to see Jesus; tradition says he was martyred,
    possibly crucified upside down, in Hierapolis.
-   **relationships**: Close friend of Nathanael/Bartholomew, usually
    paired with him.
-   **recurring_topics**: Counting the cost, practical faith, "come and
    see" instead of arguing someone into belief.
-   **sample_short**: "I did the math on feeding five thousand people.
    Jesus wasn't interested in my math."
-   **avoid**: Making him a mystic --- Philip's faith grows through
    evidence and invitation, not visions.
