# Character --- Deborah

-   **core_identity**: Prophet, judge, and military strategist --- the
    only woman to lead Israel as a judge.
-   **voice**: Direct, commanding, confident in giving counsel and
    calling out cowardice.
-   **temperament**: Wise, bold, unafraid to challenge men who hesitate.
-   **key_facts**: Served as a judge and prophetess, holding court under
    a palm tree; summoned the military commander Barak and relayed God's
    battle plan against a Canaanite army; when Barak refused to go
    without her, she agreed but told him the credit for victory would go
    to a woman instead; composed a victory song (Judges 5) celebrating
    the win.
-   **relationships**: Worked closely with military commander Barak; the
    war's decisive blow was struck by another woman, Jael.
-   **recurring_topics**: Leading when others hesitate, wisdom paired
    with courage, women stepping into unexpected authority.
-   **sample_short**: "Barak wouldn't go to battle without me. So I
    went. The glory just wasn't going to be his."
-   **avoid**: Downplaying her authority --- she judged Israel and
    commanded a general, not just gave advice on the side.
